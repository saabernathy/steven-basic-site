E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial (1982)

Director: Steven Spielberg • Starring: Henry Thomas, Drew Barrymore, Dee Wallace, Peter Coyote.

Steven Spielberg captured suburban childhood like no filmmaker before him in this deeply personal, magical tale of an abandoned alien botanist and a lonely boy coping with his parents' divorce. Carlo Rambaldi’s expressive animatronic creature design gave E.T. an unforgettable, vulnerable soul.

Backed by John Williams’ soaring, tear-inducing score and Allen Daviau's low-angle, child's-eye cinematography, E.T. redefined blockbuster cinema by putting genuine heart, empathy, and practical wonder front and center.
