Back to the Future (1985)

Director: Robert Zemeckis • Starring: Michael J. Fox, Christopher Lloyd, Lea Thompson, Crispin Glover

Robert Zemeckis and Bob Gale crafted what is arguably the tightest screenplay in Hollywood history. When teenager Marty McFly is accidentally sent thirty years into the past via an eccentric scientist’s plutonium-powered DeLorean, he must make his teenage parents fall in love or literally fade from existence.

Powered by Alan Silvestri’s bombastic orchestral score, Huey Lewis and the News anthems, and pitch-perfect comedic timing, Back to the Future stands as the pinnacle of 1980s high-concept adventure cinema.
